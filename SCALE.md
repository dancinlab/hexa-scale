## Honest caveat

The n=6 lattice (`σ(6)·φ(6) = 6·τ(6) = 24`) is **mathematically true and
unique to n=6 on `[2, 10000]`** (Monte Carlo z = 3.06, p = 0.003 vs
n=28 / n=496). The claim that *"optimal designs at all scales are
derived from this identity"* is a **research hypothesis** about
organising principles — **not a measurement**.

Per [`echoes/LATTICE_POLICY.md`](https://github.com/dancinlab/echoes/blob/main/LATTICE_POLICY.md):

- The lattice is an **organising tool** — never a substitute for real math /
  physics / engineering / political-economy limits.
- n=6 lattice-fit is **forbidden** on external entities. **NASA · SpaceX ·
  ESA · KASI · CERN · IPCC · IEEE · ASTM · ISO · UNESCO · national
  building codes · treaty bodies · individual architects / planners** use
  their own published invariants. This repo never claims those parties
  adopt the lattice — it only shows what the n=6 ladder looks like when
  applied as a *lens* to the question "how is space designed and run at
  every scale?"
- The **3 upper-scale rows (multiverse · universe · galaxy)** carry no
  manufactured artifact today and likely never will at design-by-humans
  granularity. They are tagged `⚠ speculative` throughout. The 3 lower
  rows (planet · country · city) are tagged `operational` — real
  artifacts exist; the lattice is one lens among many.

## Lattice mapping (the central identity)

```
σ(6) = 1 + 2 + 3 + 6 = 12       (sum of divisors)
τ(6) = |{ 1, 2, 3, 6 }| = 4     (count of divisors)
φ(6) = |{ 1, 5 }| = 2            (Euler totient)
J₂(6) = σ(6) · τ(6) = 12 · 4 = 24

         6 scales × 4 phases = 24 cells = J₂(6)
```

The 24-cell matrix in this repo IS the J₂(6) ladder, made visible as a
design-and-governance grid.

## §A — Scales (rows of the ladder)

### A1. Multiverse  `⚠ speculative`

- **Artifact**: none manufactured. Conceptual ensemble of universes (Tegmark Level I–IV; string landscape ≥ 10⁵⁰⁰ vacua).
- **Mainstream invariants**: anthropic selection · multiverse measure problem · Boltzmann brain bounds.
- **n=6 lens (research hypothesis only)**: parameter selection across vacua — what's the invariant the multiverse selects for? Open.

### A2. Universe  `⚠ speculative`

- **Artifact**: the observable universe (~93 Gly diameter, ~10²² stars).
- **Mainstream invariants**: Λ-CDM (`Ωₘ`, `Ωₐ`, `H₀`); Planck mass; nucleosynthesis ratios (H : He : Li ≈ 75 : 24 : 1); spectral index `nₛ`; reionisation `τ_e`.
- **n=6 lens (research hypothesis only)**: 6 epochs of expansion (inflation → reheating → radiation → matter → dark-energy → heat-death)? Suggestive but not a measurement.

### A3. Galaxy  `⚠ speculative`

- **Artifact**: galaxies in mass range 10⁶ – 10¹⁴ M☉. None *designed*.
- **Mainstream invariants**: Hubble morphology classification · Tully-Fisher relation (`L ∝ v⁴`) · stellar mass function · initial mass function (IMF; Salpeter `α=2.35`).
- **n=6 lens**: 6 spiral arms theoretically possible (most galaxies have 2–4); 6-arm galaxies exist but are rare. Not a design target.

### A4. Planet  `operational`

- **Artifact**: terraformed / engineered planet (Mars / Venus targets; not yet realised).
- **Mainstream invariants**: Bond albedo · Stefan-Boltzmann (`σ_SB T⁴`) · gravitational binding · plate-tectonics activity · greenhouse forcing.
- **n=6 lens (operational analogy)**: 6 planetary subsystems (atmosphere · hydrosphere · cryosphere · biosphere · lithosphere · magnetosphere). This is *descriptive*, not prescriptive — planetary scientists use these subsystem names for clarity, not because of n=6.

### A5. Country  `operational`

- **Artifact**: nation-states (~193 UN members + non-members).
- **Mainstream invariants**: GDP per capita · Gini · trade balance · constitutional separation of powers (legislative · executive · judicial) · sovereignty under UN charter.
- **n=6 lens**: country-level governance often clusters into 6-axis evaluations (security · economy · social · environment · diplomacy · technology) — used by think-tanks (RAND · CSIS), but not unique to n=6 (also seen as 4 / 5 / 7-axis variants).

### A6. City  `operational`

- **Artifact**: ~10,000 cities globally; ~30 megacities (>10M).
- **Mainstream invariants**: population density (people / km²) · IBC / IRC / NEC / NFPA / ISO 37120 / ICLEI metrics · zoning codes · transit accessibility.
- **n=6 lens (operational analogy)**: 6 urban subsystems (built · transport · power · water · communication · waste). Many city-planning frameworks use this exact list (Forman 2008; Marshall 2009) — again descriptive, not derived from n=6.

## §B — Phases (columns of the lifecycle)

### B1. Design

- **What**: select parameters, topology, invariants. Produce drawings · specs · simulations.
- **Receiving artifact**: drawing · spec · CAD model · simulation suite.
- **Key actors per scale**: cosmologist (multiverse / universe / galaxy) → planetary scientist (planet) → constitutional designer (country) → urban planner (city).

### B2. Build

- **What**: physical realisation · construction · deployment.
- **Receiving artifact**: building · structure · system in place.
- **Key actors per scale**: ⚠ none (multiverse · universe · galaxy) → astroengineer (planet, hypothetical) → developer · contractor (country infrastructure) → general contractor + trades (city).

### B3. Operate

- **What**: runtime · maintenance · adjustment · serving stakeholders.
- **Receiving artifact**: live system delivering function.
- **Key actors per scale**: ⚠ none (multiverse · universe · galaxy) → climate stabilisation infrastructure (planet, hypothetical) → bureaucracy · regulator · operator (country) → utility · transit · police · sanitation (city).

### B4. Policy

- **What**: rules · norms · enforcement · accountability framework.
- **Receiving artifact**: written code · treaty · constitution · regulation.
- **Key actors per scale**: ⚠ none (multiverse · universe) → first-contact / panspermia treaty bodies (galaxy, hypothetical) → planetary-boundary IPCC-class bodies (planet) → legislator · diplomat · judiciary (country) → city council · zoning board · code official (city).
